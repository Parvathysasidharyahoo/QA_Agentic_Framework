3. Key Risks (Business Critical Defects, ranked most severe first)

1. SCN-43 — False order confirmation on direct URL navigation to checkout-complete.html Application defect

Navigating directly to /checkout-complete.html after login, with no items ever added and no checkout steps completed, renders the real "Thank you for your order!" heading exactly as if a genuine order had been placed — no redirect or state guard exists. This is a customer-trust risk (a user could believe a non-existent order was placed) and directly breaks the core purchase journey's Order Confirmation step per the High/No-Go rubric example verbatim.
