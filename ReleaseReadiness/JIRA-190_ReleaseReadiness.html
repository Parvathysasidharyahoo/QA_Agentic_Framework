3. Risk assessment

  • High attention: SCN-38 (possible duplicate order). If confirmed, a double-click on Finish could place two orders. That would be a customer-facing defect in the core checkout flow. The analysis cannot settle this, at 50% confidence.
  • Medium: SCN-25 (SCRUM-12). A confirmation page can be reached without an order. This is a confirmed state-guard defect. It is lower impact on a demo-style app, but the order-confirmation flow is core to this requirement. Release needs a fix or a documented risk acceptance from the product owner.
  • Medium: SCN-5 (unverified coverage). The multi-user journey has not run to completion. Its single-user parts pass (SCN-27, SCN-28), which lowers the risk, but combined behaviour is untested.
  • Low: SCN-32 and SCN-34 (test defects). These do not point to product problems. They do leave "all six products in cart" and "injection input in shipping form" unverified until the tests are fixed. The injection scenario is a security check, so re-run it before sign-off. Login-field injection (SCN-33) passes.

What is working: the main purchase paths pass (single item, multiple items, remove from cart, post-order return with an empty cart, logout after purchase). So do login validation (invalid or empty credentials, locked-out user), required shipping-field validation, the authentication gate for direct URLs, cart persistence after refresh and after re-login, and the problem_user and performance_glitch_user single-user journeys.

5. Exit conditions for release

  1. Resolve SCN-38 by manual reproduction or a trace. If a duplicate order occurs, log a defect and fix it before release. If not, fix the test to count completed orders and not raw navigation events.
  2. Fix SCRUM-12, or get the product owner's written acceptance of the risk and update the test to a known-issue baseline.
  3. Fix the SCN-5 test (reset cart or use a fresh context between users, and tolerate a "Remove" button state). Re-run it to confirm the multi-user journey.
  4. Correct the product name in SCN-32 and filter the known 401 console noise in SCN-34. Re-run both.
  5. Re-run the full suite. Target is 35 of 35 passing, or only documented, accepted known issues.
